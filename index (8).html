    04 · Method limitations

    Clustering discovers similarity, not proven competitive rivalry or causation. The sample, business boundaries, time period, researcher-coded menu categories, feature weighting, and k-means assumptions affect results. PCA flattens high-dimensional distances into two axes and may hide separations; clusters were computed in all active dimensions, not on the 2D image. Price, taste, quality, site overlap, consumer switching, and unit economics are not measured.

    Reproducibility / 05

    Data provenance and interpretation

    Study universe: Chipotle and nine direct or adjacent fast-casual/quick-service U.S. restaurant chains. Time convention: FY2025 year-end restaurant metrics and 2024 comparatives; a few state coverage and menu observations were retrieved in 2026. How research is gathered: the default records were curated from linked public annual filings, franchise disclosure summaries, official brand announcements, official menus, and store directories. The file works offline and does not claim to scrape the web live. For a different industry: add sourced brand entries or import an appropriately researched CSV. Chart reading: a point’s position is not a price/quality score; distance is approximate after PCA projection. Decision boundary: exploratory group mapping only; do not use it to decide a store opening, acquisition, or marketing budget without current local competitive, consumer and economic research.

    Result changes are reproducible when the same dataset, feature definitions and k settings are used. Source URLs are printed as clickable links in browser-generated PDFs.
